## Hi, I'm Nafis Karim

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago. I work on web
security and the security of AI agents: how LLM-powered browser agents behave on the web,
how to tell them apart from real users, and how to keep them from being abused. Most of my
work is measurement-driven.

Before UIC I was a lecturer at BRAC University, and I did my B.Sc. in Computer Science and
Engineering at BUET.

### Research interests

Agentic AI security, behavioral and browser fingerprinting, web security, and AI agent
detection.

### Selected work

- **AgentShield: Fortifying Web Applications Against AI Agents**, accepted to IEEE S&P 2027
  (with shepherding).
- [AgentShield-ContentModification-Package](https://github.com/NafisMahi/AgentShield-ContentModification-Package):
  a tool from the AgentShield project that reads a site's own content and embeds hidden,
  agent-directed prompts in its served pages, for indirect prompt-injection research on
  sites you control.
